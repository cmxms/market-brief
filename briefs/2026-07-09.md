Short-gamma means dealers hedge *with* the move, not against it — so trends extend and both directions can get violent once a wall breaks. Walls to watch: **SPY 750 call / 740 put**, **QQQ 720 call / 700 put**. Inside those, expect chop that pays fades; a clean break through a wall is where short-gamma acceleration shows up and fading gets punished. With spot sitting essentially *on* the SPY flip, this is a knife's-edge regime — small moves flip the sign of dealer hedging, so intraday direction is unusually reflexive here.
