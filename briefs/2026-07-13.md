Watch that split: if SMH starts to crack too, the whole tech complex is in trouble.

## 🧭 Regime read
Geopolitics is driving the tape, but the internals aren't broken. Dealer gamma is **positive** on both SPY and QQQ — meaning dealers dampen moves and dips tend to get bought, with SPY's zero-gamma flip down at **~752** (spot ~755) and QQQ's near **~722** (spot ~726). Those flips are the line in the sand: above them, expect chop-and-recover; a break below flips dealers to *amplifying* selling. Call resistance sits at SPY **757 / 760**, support at **750**. Net: a jittery, headline-driven session with a buy-the-dip cushion intact — but keep size small into a CPI print that lands tomorrow morning and an oil story that can gap on a single Hormuz headline.
