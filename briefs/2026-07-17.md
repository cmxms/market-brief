## 📰 Last 24h — what happened & why

**The chip de-rating is the story, and TSMC is the proof.** TSMC reported a record Q2 — $40.2B revenue (+36% y/y), EPS $4.31 vs $3.89 expected, gross margin 67.7%, and raised full-year growth guidance above 40% with Q3 guided as high as $45.8B. The stock fell ~3.3% anyway. The Semiconductor Index has now shed >20% from its June peak in three weeks.

That combination is the signal: nobody is disputing AI *demand*. What's being repriced is how long today's scarcity, margins and capex intensity can last. Add Samsung's results and reports that China's DeepSeek is building its own AI chip, and the crowded side of the trade is getting trimmed.

**War premium is back in oil.** The US carried out a 6th consecutive night of strikes on Iran, extending to bridges and an airport; Iran struck eastern Syria. Strait of Hormuz traffic has collapsed — ~6 vessels crossed in a 12-hour window vs 18-22/day earlier this month and ~130/day pre-war. A tanker was seized off Yemen. Treasury's authorization for Iranian oil sales lapsed at 12:01am ET **today**. WTI +1.38% to $80.04.

**The counterweight:** Thursday's US data was solid — firm core retail sales, jobless claims fell, Northeast manufacturing surged. Gold is heading for its biggest weekly loss in six. And yields fell. That is not a tape in flight-to-safety.

```

     flip 750.78  |  call wall 755   put wall 750

     flip 705.89  |  call wall 720   put wall 700
     walls: 690 / 695 / 700 / 705 / 709
     full-chain: flip 705.94, -5.13bn, call wall 730
```

**Both are opening on a knife's edge.** SPY sits 6 cents *under* its flip; QQQ sits 5 cents *over* its. That's as close to the pivot as this gets, and near-term and structural flips agree (750.78/750.85, 705.89/705.94) — there's no divergence to arbitrage, the whole chain says the same thing.

**Negative gamma is the operative condition.** Dealers are short gamma on both, so their hedging works *with* price rather than against it — they sell into weakness and buy into strength. The practical consequence: moves tend to extend rather than mean-revert, ranges break instead of holding, and **mean-reversion fades get punished** — which is precisely the condition in which a "it's down enough" dip-buy is most dangerous. Directional continuation is favored over rotation-back-to-value.

Sitting *exactly on the flip* means the sign of that amplification isn't set yet. Above it, dealer hedging turns stabilizing and the tape calms; below it, hedging feeds the move and air pockets open. SPY's put wall at 750 is *at spot* — the first real shelf; the next is 740. QQQ has 700 (a round number as well as the put wall) roughly 0.8% below.

**OPEX caveat — this structure has an expiry date, today.** A large share of this gamma rolls off at the cash close. Pinning pressure near these strikes is strongest into the close, and then the constraint releases: Monday's regime can look materially different from today's on the same price. Whatever gets read into today's levels shouldn't be extrapolated into next week without re-pulling the chain.
