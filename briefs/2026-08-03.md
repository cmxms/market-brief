## 📰 Last 24h — what happened & why
- **Diplomacy replaced a strike.** Trump cancelled what he called the biggest attack since WWII, saying talks begin today and that a deal means the "immediate, complete and total opening of the Hormuz Strait" plus an end to Iran's nuclear program. Gulf allies, Saudi Arabia especially, reportedly pushed for the pause.
- **Iran contradicted it hours later.** A foreign-ministry spokesman said "we currently do not have negotiations with America," with no delegations planned either way; Tehran is talking to **Oman** about a *temporary* safe passage and says Hormuz won't return to its pre-war status. **The relief move is priced off talks one side says aren't happening.**
- **Crude did the repricing:** WTI **−6.8% to $78.89**, Brent near $83. Treasuries bid, gold flat.
- **Friday's backdrop:** S&P +0.7% (7,489.72), Nasdaq +1.0%, Dow +0.5% — Amazon surged on earnings, Apple sank on guidance, yields *rose* on inflation worry. July was strong (Dow and Nasdaq ~+9%, Russell +18%) and earnings are good: ~300 S&P names in, **85% beating**.
