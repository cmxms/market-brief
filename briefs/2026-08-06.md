## 📰 Last 24h — what happened & why
- **Claims 199k vs 202k expected** (prior 198k), 4-week average down to 198.75k — still near a 57-year low. The softer detail: **continuing claims rose to 1.801M, above the 1.790M expected.** Few people are being fired; those who lose a job take longer to find the next.
- **Productivity was the best number of the morning.** Q2 output per hour **+1.4% vs +0.6% expected**, with **unit labour costs +1.3% against +2.1%**. More output per hour at a lower cost per unit is what disinflation looks like in the data — and it lands **six days before CPI**.
- **Challenger: 33,429 announced job cuts in July, a two-year low**, −27% from June, with hiring plans the strongest July on record. **AI was the most-cited reason for the fifth straight month** — a healthy labour market with one force steadily eating jobs inside it.
- **Iran cuts both ways.** Tehran cited progress with Oman on a Strait of Hormuz arrangement, and European shares hit a record peak partly on that. Against it: Houthis claim strikes on two Saudi tankers, Gulf shipping is down, Israel struck south Lebanon. A peace bid and an escalation bid at once. Also today: **SpaceX's first IPO lockup expires**, freeing 900M+ shares.
