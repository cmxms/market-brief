# 📊 Market Brief — Fri August 7, 2026
*Data pulled Fri Aug 07, 2026 9:01 AM ET*

**TL;DR:** July payrolls **fell 23,000** against a **+83k** consensus — the first outright drop in months — and the prior two months were revised down a combined **103,000**. Unemployment ticked *down* to 4.1%, but only because participation fell to a five-year low. The market had been pricing a September Fed **hike**; that's now off the table. Yields fell across the curve and growth led.

## 📰 Last 24h — what happened & why
- **Payrolls −23k vs +83k expected** — the first outright drop in months. Worse than the headline: **May and June were revised down 103,000 combined**, June cut to roughly +20k. The labor market wasn't just soft in July; it was softer than reported all along.
- **The 4.1% unemployment rate is a bad print, not a good one.** It fell from 4.2% because **participation dropped to 61.4%**, a five-year low — people leaving the workforce, not finding work. **Temporary layoffs rose 153,000 to 921,000.**
- **This broke the hike trade.** Fed funds sit at **3.50–3.75%**, and going in, most futures traders priced a quarter-point **hike** in September. Post-print, the majority expects a hold. That repricing — not the jobs number itself — is what's moving the tape.
- **Oil keeps falling on peace headlines.** WTI **$76.82 (−0.6%)**, down ~12% in five sessions as Iran and Oman near a Strait of Hormuz navigation deal. The open issue is the transit fee: Iran wants 5–7% of cargo value, Oman ~3%, Washington zero.

## 🧭 Regime read (generalized)
- **SPY and QQQ are in opposite gamma regimes.** SPY at ~768.6 sits **right on its near-term flip at ~769.3** — a knife edge, dealer gamma marginally negative. QQQ at ~714.7 is **well above its flip (~705.9)** with positive gamma.
