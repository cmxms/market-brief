And the S&P's **+6% in 12 sessions** to a record rests on earnings that may flatter — revenue from AI-partner investment and data-center buildouts funded by forgone free cash flow, plus a one-off wartime surge in energy profits.

Two indices, two regimes, and the leading one is the pinned one — which caps how far a narrow AI-led bid can carry the tape.
